## Brand & Style

The design system establishes an atmospheric, contemplative sanctuary grounded in ultra-dark luxury minimalism. Designed for high-end digital wellness, cognitive introspection, and neural health applications, it speaks to mindful, discerning users seeking calm clarity amidst cognitive overload.

The aesthetic fuses celestial eclipse phenomena with bioluminescent neural circuitry. Organic spherical gradients, diffuse copper and amber auras, and illuminated rim borders emerge gracefully from absolute pitch-black canvases. The tone balances deep stillness with vibrant mental vitality:
- **Introspective Depth:** Pitch-black canvases (#040507) prevent visual fatigue and create an intimate, infinite negative space.
- **Ethereal Bioluminescence:** Radiant crescent lighting and delicate neural nodes ignite focal points without producing harsh glare.
- **Sculpted Tactility:** Glowing pill controls, concentric orbital tracks, and dark glass card surfaces feel physically carved and magnetically charged.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around radiant lunar thermodynamics set against a deep cosmic void. 

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Amber Crescent (`#FF8A3D`):** The signature vibrant copper glow, used for primary action states, focal auras, and key interactive highlights.
- **Secondary Neural Spark (`#F59E0B`):** Bioluminescent gold employed for neural nodes, fine vector connection strokes, and secondary accents.
- **Tertiary Burnt Bronze (`#92400E`):** Low-frequency thermal anchor used for deep gradient stops, subtle ring shadows, and inactive boundary definitions.
- **Neutral Deep Pitch (`#07090E` / `#040507`):** The foundational substrate, absorbing ambient light and allowing illuminated elements to float with optical separation.

### Functional Tinting & Glows
- **Luminous Edge Strokes:** Semi-transparent linear highlights (`linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(255, 138, 61, 0.45) 0%, rgba(255, 138, 61, 0.05) 100%)`) define physical borders.
- **Aura Radial Gradients:** Extended background blurs utilizing soft spherical radials (`radial-gradient(circle, rgba(232, 114, 40, 0.28) 0%, rgba(7, 9, 14, 0) 70%)`) to create the characteristic eclipse rim atmosphere.
- **Text Contrast System:** Pure high-contrast stark white (`#FFFFFF`) for primary headings, warming down to muted amber-sand (`#D1A98E`) for secondary supporting text, and soft charcoal (`#64748B`) for disabled or tertiary captions.

## Typography

The typography system relies on **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all levels. Its balanced geometric construction and subtle humanist terminal curves balance technical precision with warm approachability.

- **Headlines:** Set in high-contrast solid white (`#FFFFFF`) with tight negative tracking to maintain bold punch and authority against dark spatial backgrounds. On hero onboarding sequences, major titles incorporate a diffuse amber text-shadow (`text-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(255, 138, 61, 0.35)`).
- **Body & Paragraphs:** Rendered in softened bone and warm sand tones (`rgba(253, 186, 116, 0.85)` or `#CBD5E1`) with generous line-heights to ensure effortless legibility.
- **Labels & Overlines:** Tracked out, rendered in medium to semibold weights, frequently paired with pill-shaped translucent micro-badges to organize conversational flows.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around deliberate vertical pacing and centered focal balance, drawing the eye toward cosmic glowing rings, crescent light arcs, and sculpted bottom-sheet action targets.

- **Grid Architecture:** 
  - Mobile employs a single-column, fluid stack with `1.25rem` margins and `1rem` gutters.
  - Tablet and desktop expand to an 8 or 12-column centered layout constrained to a maximum content width of 680px for focused conversational flows, or 1120px for dashboard data representations.
- **Vertical Hierarchy:** Large expressive negative spaces (`space-2xl`) surround visual hero centers (e.g., celestial eclipse illustrations, neural maps), creating a spacious temple-like breathing room before interactive controls begin.
- **Safe Area Anchoring:** Primary action pills and sliding controls anchor consistently to the viewport footer, framed with subtle upward-diffusing black-to-transparent scrims.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed entirely through **tonal luminescence, specular rim highlights, and atmospheric glassmorphism**, rejecting conventional flat gray shadows.

### Surface Elevation Layers
1. **Base Cosmic Void (0dp):** Pitch black `#040507` baseline canvas.
2. **Atmospheric Aura Layer (1dp):** Backing celestial discs, eclipse crescent blends, and particle network lines suspended in deep field blur (`blur(32px)` to `blur(64px)`).
3. **Mantle Surface Cards (2dp):** Charcoal obsidian `#0D1017` with 60% opacity, layered over `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` and framed with a dual stroke: an illuminated copper-to-transparent top border (`rgba(255, 138, 61, 0.3)`) and an ultra-subtle base border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`).
4. **Interactive Kinetic Floating (3dp):** Elevated glowing pills and focus modules feature inner radial reflections and soft external amber drop auras (`box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(232, 114, 40, 0.45), 0 0 16px rgba(255, 138, 61, 0.25)`).

### Rim Light Mechanics
Surfaces mimic edge lighting: top outer edges catch radiant copper light from overhead lunar crescents, while lower edges fade softly into background void shadows.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a fluid, pill-centric geometry (Level 3), mirroring orbital ellipses and gentle human biological forms.

- **Primary Interactive Targets:** Full continuous stadium/pill geometries (`border-radius: 9999px`) provide tactile, pebble-like ergonomics for buttons, input triggers, and slider nubs.
- **Cards & Surfaces:** Rounded with generous 24px (`1.5rem`) to 32px (`2rem`) radiuses to evoke polished obsidian pebbles.
- **Concentric Orbits:** Graphic features and progress states utilize nested concentric circular paths, lending astronomical precision to progress indications.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Eclipse Glow Button:** Full pill shape, layered with an internal top-to-bottom copper gradient (`linear-gradient(180deg, #E87228 0%, #78350F 100%)`), framed with a crisp illuminated top border (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.35)`), and accented by an external radial amber halo. Centered bold text in pure white.
- **Secondary Ghost Pill Button:** Translucent base (`rgba(13, 16, 23, 0.6)`) surrounded by a hairline contour (`1px solid rgba(255, 138, 61, 0.25)`). Hover states elevate border brightness to 60% opacity with an inner amber mist.
- **Orbital Sliding Action:** Vertical pill capsule with chevron guides and a glowing circular thumb slider ('Go'), simulating physical celestial docking.

### Input Fields
- **Obsidian Recessed Fields:** Heightened pill container (`52px` height) with a deeply recessed dark fill (`rgba(10, 13, 19, 0.7)`).
- **Hairline Border:** Resting border at `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. On focus, the border transitions smoothly into glowing copper (`1px solid #FF8A3D`) with a soft localized amber diffuse glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 12px rgba(255, 138, 61, 0.2)`).
- **Placeholder & Text:** Inputs use `body-md` in warm muted sand (`#94A3B8`), shifting to crisp `#FFFFFF` upon typing.

### Chips & Micro-Badges
- **Translucent Assistant Badges:** Compact pills with dark smoke fill (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`), subtle hairline perimeter, and micro-typography in muted copper (`#FDBA74`). Used for section categorizations, assistant indicators, and conversational tags.

### Neural Cards & Interactive Containers
- **Frosted Eclipse Cards:** 24px border radius with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and ultra-subtle amber rim lighting.
- **Social & Quick Action Circles:** Pill or perfect circular buttons housing identity icons with ambient backlit glows on touch interactions.

### Neural Node & Particle Visualizers
- **Constellation Connectors:** Hairline vector lines (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.15)`) connecting golden glowing junction nodes (`#F59E0B`), accented with multi-tier radial bloom rings to represent cognitive links and energetic states.
